**The bug:** in watch mode, the per-app app-details snapshot (`pendingAppRecord`) is pushed into the dataset and counted in `pushed` exactly like a review row -- but both the status message and the `webhookUrl` payload derived "new reviews" as `pushed - watchChanged`, which silently counted that app-detail row itself as a phantom "new review" whenever `includeAppDetails` (default **true**) was combined with `watchLabel` and at least one app had any delivery. First-ever test of this combination; all prior watch-mode `varied_test` passes (c2207, c2175) never combined `includeAppDetails:true` watch deliveries with a count-correctness check.
